Today, we are announcing the shutdown of Aosus Services.

Aosus Services offered alternative frontends for platforms such as Twitter and YouTube, allowing you to access their content while preserving your privacy and blocking trackers.

Unfortunately, recent changes by platforms to combat ai scrappers have made these frontends increasingly unreliable. Fixing them has also become difficult and requires significant time, effort, and even financial resources.

As a result, we have decided to shut down Aosus services and redirect the available server resources toward improving Aosus, for example, by dedicating capacity to ai agents for internal usage.

Thank you for supporting Aosus.
